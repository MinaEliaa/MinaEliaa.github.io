D360 Bank Live

D360 Bank: turning a slogan-led website into a clear path to the app

Client
D360 Bank
Year
2026
Industry
Digital banking · Riyadh, Saudi Arabia
Platform
Marketing website (English & Arabic)
View websiteView case study on Behance

Overview

D360 Bank is a Saudi digital bank that lets people open an account in 2 minutes, then pay, save and send money abroad, all fully Shariah-compliant. This conversion-rate UX audit and redesign turns the website into a simple, trustworthy route to the app: real stats, genuine trust signals and one clear next step.

MissionMaking the D360 website a simple, trustworthy route to the app: real stats, genuine trust signals, and one clear next step.

Problem / Goal

  • The products are solid, but the website buries them under slogans.
  • The new site should focus on one clear action.
  • It should include genuine trust signals.
  • It should showcase every product upfront.

What I did

  • UX audit
  • First-time visitor journey
  • Conversion-rate analysis
  • Problems & solutions mapping
  • Information architecture
  • UI redesign
  • Arabic RTL & English
  • Responsive web
  • Front-end build

The products are strong, but the site gives visitors no clear way to download the app.

Problems & solutions

Turning a slogan-led website into a clear path to the app.

  1. 01 Problem

    The only sign-up button, “Start your journey”, shows a bare QR code on desktop and jumps straight to Google Play on Android.

    01 Solution

    One clear “Get the App” button, repeated through the page, with App Store and Google Play badges.

  2. 02 Problem

    Every claim, like “best rate” or “no hidden fees”, is a slogan with no numbers behind it.

    02 Solution

    Benefits with real numbers: 2 minutes, 100% Shariah, SAR 0 fees, 4.49% APR and up to 3% profit.

  3. 03 Problem

    Trust breaks early: the customer count literally reads 3 trillion, and SAMA regulation is buried in the footer.

    03 Solution

    Trust up front: a 4.8 rating from 41.5K reviews and 3M+ customers right next to the app.

  4. 04 Problem

    The homepage sells travel only, while financing, savings and Musaned never appear.

    04 Solution

    Every product shown up front: cards, transfers, financing, Sanabil savings and Musaned, each in its own tile.

  5. 05 Problem

    The three homepage “Learn More” links send English visitors to the Arabic site.

    05 Solution

    Language stays consistent: one EN / العربية switch in the header, and every section exists in both languages.

  6. 06 Problem

    On mobile the button is cut off, followed by about 1,500px of near-empty scrolling, and the logo disappears.

    06 Solution

    A compact mobile hero: headline, button and cards fit the first screen, and every scroll shows real content.

Our approach

Five ideas shaped the new website.

  • Numbers, not slogans

    Every benefit is a real figure: 2 minutes, SAR 0 fees, 4.49% APR, up to 3% profit. Visitors can compare D360 in seconds.

  • Arabic first

    Built right to left from the start, with a full English version and a one-tap language switch.

  • Personal and playful

    A card colour picker and the Pink Card campaign (for Saudi women) make the bank feel made for you.

  • One clear action

    A single “Get the App” button, repeated through the page, with App Store and Google Play badges right under it.

  • Trust where you decide

    The 4.8 rating, 41.5K reviews and 3M+ customers sit right next to the button, not buried in the footer.

AxisPay Workshop

AxisPay: a rewards ecosystem that keeps salary users coming back

Client
AxisPay
Year
2026
Industry
Fintech · Digital wallet
Platform
Mobile application (iOS & Android)
View case study on Behance

Overview

A UI/UX redesign of the AxisPay salary wallet, built around a retention-focused rewards ecosystem. Everyday payments earn points that users redeem for bills, cashback and partner vouchers, turning a pay-day tool into a daily habit.

Problem / Goal

  • Missing reasons to stay: AxisPay felt like a basic tool, not an exciting financial app.
  • Users logged in only to transfer or withdraw their salary, then left right away.
  • No emotional connection, hidden features and no daily habits.
  • Goal: turn passive users into daily active users with clear reward goals.
  • Make everyday actions like paying bills fun, without hurting the company’s profits or letting users game the rewards.

What I did

  • Understanding the challenge
  • Product & community research
  • User insights
  • Competitive analysis
  • Feature exploration
  • Sitemap & UX logic
  • Design system
  • UI revamp
  • Prototype
  • Presentation

Research insights

  • 60–70%of users only receive their salary in AxisPay, then stop using it after payday
  • 4pain points: functional-only experience, inconsistent UI, zero rewards visibility, no gamification
  • 3competitors analysed: Telda, Klivvr and Lucky

Wish Live

Wish: connect, share and save your dream wishes with friends

Client
Wish mobile app · Algorithma, KSA
Year
2026
Industry
Social networking
Platform
Mobile application (iOS & Android)
View case study on BehanceDownload on theApp Store

Overview

Wish is the first-of-its-kind social wishlist platform in Saudi Arabia: a mobile app that redefines how people share desires and celebrate each other’s preferences. Its mission is to create a meaningful experience that transforms how people express care, support and celebration through gifting, fostering deeper relationships and removing the stress of choosing the perfect gift.

MissionBuilt for emotionally connected users who enjoy expressing care through giving: young adults and professionals, close-knit friend groups, socially active users who gift for birthdays, Eid and anniversaries, and digital-first people looking for seamless, meaningful gifting.

Problem / Goal

  • Most people only tell friends what they want in conversation (60%), so gifts are a guess.
  • Half of gift givers ask directly, and 30% guess based on interests, which often means duplicate or unwanted presents.
  • Goal: blend social interaction with personalised wish sharing, so users can create public or private wishlists, post updates and engage with their circles.
  • Let friends reserve a wish and earn achievement badges, so there is no more guessing, no more duplicate presents, and gifting becomes intentional, emotional and fun.
