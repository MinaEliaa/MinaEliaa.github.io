Frequently asked questions

Still unsure? Message us on WhatsApp and a real person replies.

How big are the portions?

Standard portions are 120 g protein (8 pieces of shrimp) and 200 g carbs. Less carbs is free; more is priced by Gutty.

Where do you deliver?

Greater Cairo, Giza, 6th of October and Hadayek October, Sheikh Zayed, New Cairo and Alexandria up to Agami. For other areas, message Gutty before subscribing.

Can I pause my plan?

Yes. Freeze up to 14 days a month with a day's notice, and your subscription extends by the same number of days.

How much is delivery?

Delivery fees depend on your area and are confirmed by Gutty.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are possible only within the first 2 weeks.

How is my calorie target set?

From your sex, age, height, weight, activity level (desk job, training 2 to 4 times a week, or 5 or more) and pace: slow, moderate or hard.

What do I need to subscribe?

Your full name, WhatsApp number, full address, area and preferred start date, plus any allergies or dislikes. Email is optional.

How do I pay?

Online by Visa, Mastercard, wallet, Fawry or InstaPay. Your subscription is tied to your registered name and address.

About Gutty. Gutty is a healthy-food kitchen in Egypt that cooks sourdough burgers, chicken and beef bowls, breakfast, salads and sugar-free sweets, sized to each customer’s calorie target and delivered daily. Weekly and monthly plans start from EGP 278 a meal. Plans can be frozen up to 14 days a month, and refunds are possible within the first 2 weeks.
